## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-stakes, bespoke investigative atmosphere termed "Editorial Intelligence." Rather than leaning into cliché Hollywood-style neon cyber tropes, it adopts the gravity of confidential global briefings, forensic journalism, and defense-grade analytics. The visual voice is authoritative, austere, and surgically precise.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Engineers, intelligence directors, financial crime analysts, and forensic investigators operate in high-density environments. The interface evokes:
- **Quiet Authority:** Zero visual noise, garish glow filters, or frivolous animations.
- **Intellectual Clarity:** Deep black ink canvases paired with generous negative space allow critical indicators to demand instant focus without causing ocular fatigue.
- **Physical Precision:** Optical frosted glass sheets, refined hair-thin borders, and crisp tactile telemetry.

### Design Movement & Influences
- **Editorial Structuralism:** Grand, literary serif headlines evoke investigative reports and classified files.
- **Tactile Glassmorphism:** Restrained, ultra-low-opacity glass panels layered over void-black field tones create a sense of structured spatial hierarchy.
- **Functional Utilitarianism:** High-density monospaced telemetry for cryptographic hashes, IP routes, timestamps, and financial ledgers.

## Colors

The palette rejects standard cyber conventions (no neon cyan or electric violet) in favor of warm, analog-rooted high-contrast inks, amber warnings, and targeted telemetry cues.

### Core Canvas & Foundation
- **Ink Primary (`#07090D`):** The foundational absolute base canvas. Absorbs glare, framing all data planes.
- **Ink Secondary (`#0D1117`):** Recessed containers, structural wells, and structural sidebar planes.

### Signal Accents
- **Amber / Saffron (`#F2A33A`):** The singular focal point. Reserved for active states, key threat markers, focused states, and definitive case progression.
- **Teal Telemetry (`#3FB6A8`):** Data secondary. Used strictly for confirmed pathways, verified entity links, and secondary telemetry graphs.
- **Crimson Hazard (`#E5484D`):** Risk vectors, high-priority vulnerabilities, active breaches, and destructive actions.

### Editorial Typography & Surface Shading
- **Warm Cream (`#ECE8DF`):** Primary text and headline color. Softens extreme contrast against the dark background while maintaining maximum legibility.
- **Muted Steel (`#8B93A1`):** Secondary meta-labels, table headers, inactive timestamps, and contextual borders.
- **Glass Plate Fill:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.045)`
- **Glass Stroke Perimeter:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`

## Typography

The typography creates dynamic tension between archival editorial gravitas and strict algorithmic utility.

### Role Distribution
- **Serif (Headlines & Dossier Titles):** Applied to major case markers, subject titles, and section introductions. Uses deliberate italic flourishes sparingly to indicate classified codenames or critical case metadata.
- **Grotesque Sans (General Interface & Body):** High-efficiency neutral legibility for analytical dossiers, narrative context, and interface actions.
- **Monospace (Telemetry & Cryptography):** Strictly assigned to machine outputs: IPv4/IPv6 addresses, SHA-256 signatures, currency values, latitude/longitude, and Unix epoch logs. Numbers are rendered with tabular sizing to enforce numerical alignment across lists.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column analytical canvas that prioritizes generous breathing room alongside modular density.

### Breakpoints & Structural Adaptation
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column grid. Left vertical rail (64px collapsed, 260px expanded) for case navigation, central dossier canvas spanning 8 columns, and secondary intelligence telemetry tray spanning 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Telemetry tray stacks beneath the main analytical dossier or docks into a collapsible bottom glass sheet.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Horizontal scrolling data strips for telemetry tables; cards convert to single-column vertical stacks.

### Internal Spacing Rhythm
- **Dossier Padding:** Core glass panels enforce internal padding of `space-xl` (2.5rem) on desktop to guarantee an unhurried, editorial feel.
- **Component Tightness:** Data tables, chip groups, and parameter blocks use micro-intervals (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to balance overall breathing room with high-density data legibility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft diffuse drop-shadows or high-contrast neon borders. Instead, depth is established via optical glass stratification and refined surface blurs.

### Surface Stratification

- **Level 0 (The Deep Ink Canvas):** `#07090D` solid background with an optional 0.5px subtle dot-matrix coordinate guide (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` on 32px centers).
- **Level 1 (Structural Inset Panes):** `#0D1117` surfaces used for secondary docks, data troughs, and persistent sidebars.
- **Level 2 (Glass Dossier Panels):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.045)` backdrop fill paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a crisp hairline perimeter stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Inspector Trays & Overlays):** `rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered by `rgba(242, 163, 58, 0.25)` when active or targeted, and supported by a subtle shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on a 20px curvature across all dominant structural cards and panels, softened by intentional internal precision corners for tactical elements.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Primary Dossier Panels & Main Cards:** Fixed 20px radius (`1.25rem`), creating polished, lens-like floating plates.
- **Action Buttons & Form Fields:** 8px to 10px radius (`0.5rem` to `0.625rem`) for deliberate tactile control targets.
- **Telemetry Chips & Meta Tags:** 4px radius (`0.25rem`) or fully squared brackets to reinforce machine precision within rounded glass containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Saffron Core):** `#F2A33A` solid fill with `#07090D` text in semi-bold Inter. No drop shadows. Hover: `#D98E28`. Active: subtle inset depression.
- **Secondary Glass Action:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.045)` fill, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border, `#ECE8DF` text. Hover: border shifts to `rgba(242, 163, 58, 0.5)`, background increases to `0.08` opacity.
- **Terminal Action:** Ghost layout with Monospace labels flanked by directional bracket carets (`[ ENTER EXECUTION ]`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** JetBrains Mono uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em.
  - *Active Threat:* Crimson background `rgba(229, 72, 77, 0.12)`, text `#E5484D`, border `rgba(229, 72, 77, 0.3)`.
  - *Verified Route:* Teal background `rgba(63, 182, 168, 0.12)`, text `#3FB6A8`, border `rgba(63, 182, 168, 0.3)`.
  - *Pending Analysis:* Amber background `rgba(242, 163, 58, 0.12)`, text `#F2A33A`, border `rgba(242, 163, 58, 0.3)`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Framed in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` over an `#0D1117` base. Placeholder text rendered in muted steel (`#8B93A1`). Focus state drops background transparency and introduces a crisp `1px solid #F2A33A` hairline with zero diffuse outer glow ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square/circular indicators with a 1px steel border. Checked state displays solid saffron `#F2A33A` fill with `#07090D` glyph.

### Dossier Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Encapsulated in the signature 20px rounded glass panel with 24px backdrop blur.
- **Header:** Features an editorial serif title alongside a right-aligned monospaced classification timestamp (`UTC+0`).
- **Dividers:** Low-contrast horizontal rules: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.

### Specialized Telemetry Lists
- Data rows feature alternating zero-fill and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.015)` backgrounds. Hover triggers a subtle saffron edge-border on the left boundary (2px) accompanied by text illumination from `#8B93A1` to `#ECE8DF`.
